### Personal versus caravan load

- Personal WWN packages should cover worn armor, ready weapons, a backpack, immediate adventuring tools, and the character's own short-term necessities.
- UVG sacks should hold group water, provisions, feed, trade cargo, camp equipment, bulky spare parts, medical resupply, and other caravan-scale goods.
- Do not count a character's WWN personal items again as UVG cargo unless the item is deliberately transferred to caravan storage.
- Final caravan cargo cannot be optimized until the starting vehicle/mounts, budget, departure point, route, and planned trade goods are known.
