# UVG + WWN Traditionless Album Magic

## Design principle

Mages do not choose a magical tradition. They become specialists through the spells they actually collect and master.

Spell albums are the UVG-fictional form of WWN grimoires: vinyl records, crystal harmonics, mnemonic parasites, encoded prayer wheels, illustrated manuals, fungal symphonies, or any other object that can teach reality a particular trick.

The spell mechanics remain WWN mechanics. UVG spell descriptions must be converted to a WWN spell level, range, duration, saving throw, and effect before they enter play. Do not import UVG Life costs or overcharge rules directly into the WWN chassis.

## 1. The Album Mage chassis

Use the **High Mage progression** without requiring the character to belong to the High Mage tradition.

### Full Album Mage

- Uses the Full High Mage spell-casting, spell-preparation, and Art progression.
- Gains Magic-0 as a bonus skill.
- Begins with four level-1 spells in a personal **Primer Album**.
- Begins with two general casting Techniques chosen from the approved High Mage Arts list.
- Cannot cast spells or use Techniques while wearing armor, wearing bulky clothing, or holding a shield unless a feature such as Armored Magic permits it.

### Partial Album Mage

- Uses the Partial High Mage spell-casting, spell-preparation, and Art progression.
- Gains Magic-0 as a bonus skill.
- Begins with two level-1 spells in a personal Primer Album.
- Begins with one general casting Technique chosen from the approved High Mage Arts list.
- Uses the same armor and shield restrictions as a Full Album Mage.

The GM may later introduce rare album Techniques adapted from Elementalist, Necromancer, or other Arts. These are not automatically available merely because a character has learned spells with a similar theme. Healing Touch and other class-defining Healer Arts should remain special rewards or require a dedicated character option.

## 2. Albums and spell mastery

An album can contain one or more spell tracks. Each track records:

- Spell name and WWN spell level
- One primary Resonance and, when appropriate, one secondary Resonance
- Whether the track is complete, damaged, encrypted, or flawed
- Any object, drug, sacrifice, instrument, or environmental condition required to study it

A Mage may study a spell only if its level is no higher than the maximum spell level they can cast. After mastering it, the Mage creates a personalized version in their Primer Album. The original album is no longer required for ordinary preparation, though losing the Primer Album prevents the character from changing prepared spells until it is recovered or reconstructed.

Merely owning an album does not mean its spells are known. Only mastered spells count toward Resonance.

## 3. Resonances

Resonances are descriptive families, not organizations or exclusive schools. A spell can belong to two Resonances, allowing unusual spells to bridge bodies of knowledge.

Recommended starting Resonances:

| Resonance | Typical effects |
|---|---|
| Necromancy | Corpses, death, undeath, soul extraction, post-mortality |
| Vitality | Healing, disease, poison, flesh, growth, bodily alteration |
| Mind | Memory, perception, emotion, identity, illusion, communication |
| Elements | Fire, cold, water, earth, air, weather, light, radiation |
| Artifice | Objects, machines, repair, making, transmutation, enchantment |
| Passage | Movement, force, distance, portals, summoning, time |

The GM may add or rename a Resonance when an album presents a genuinely new magical language. Avoid splitting the list so finely that no character can build familiarity.

### Resonance rating

For each Resonance, count the mastered spells bearing that tag.

| Mastered spells | Familiarity | Study modifier |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0 | Foreign | -1 |
| 1-2 | Familiar | +0 |
| 3-5 | Fluent | +1 |
| 6+ | Saturated | +2 |

If a spell has two Resonances, use the character's better rating. This lets bridge spells open a path into unfamiliar magic.

## 4. Learning a spell

WWN normally requires one week per spell level, reduced by one week per level of Magic skill, with a minimum of one day. Album Magic replaces the Magic-skill reduction with an **effective study rating**:

`Effective study rating = Magic skill + Resonance study modifier`

`Learning time in weeks = spell level - effective study rating`

If the result is zero or less, learning still takes one full day.

No roll is normally required. Time, access to the album, and the character's maximum castable spell level are the limiting factors.

### Example

Torron has Magic-0, three mastered Necromancy spells, and no Vitality spells.

- Necromancy is Fluent, giving a +1 study modifier. A new level-1 Necromancy spell takes one day.
- Vitality is Foreign, giving a -1 study modifier. A new level-1 healing spell takes two weeks.
- After Torron masters that first Vitality spell, Vitality becomes Familiar. His next level-1 Vitality spell takes the normal one week.

This creates specialization through play without forbidding cross-disciplinary learning.

## 5. Level advancement and discovered albums

The standard High Mage progression grants new spells with level advancement. To keep found albums important, convert those automatic spells into **Mastery credits**:

- A Full Album Mage gains two Mastery credits whenever their class would normally grant automatic new spells.
- A Partial Album Mage gains one Mastery credit at the same time.
- A credit can be spent only on a spell the character has encountered in an album or learned from a tutor.
- Spending a credit reduces the learning time for that spell to one day.
- Credits can be saved until a suitable album is found.

Mages may still learn additional spells through ordinary study time without spending credits, just as WWN Mages can learn from captured grimoires.

This preserves the value of Mage advancement without allowing a character to invent spells that have never appeared in the campaign.

## 6. Casting mastered spells

Mastered and prepared spells use the normal WWN rules:

- No casting check is required.
- Resonance does not change spell damage, saving throws, number of castings, or preparation capacity.
- A character may prepare any mastered spell they are capable of casting.
- Resonance makes a field easier to learn; it does not make combat casting automatically stronger.

This is the recommended core rule. It preserves the reliability and balance of the WWN Mage chassis.

## 7. Optional rule: playing an unmastered track

If the group wants albums to be immediately tempting, a Mage may attempt to cast a spell directly from an album before mastering it.

- The spell must be no higher than the Mage's maximum castable spell level.
- The Mage must have the album in hand and be able to operate it.
- Playback takes 10 minutes per spell level and cannot be performed during combat.
- The attempt consumes one of the Mage's available spell castings for the day whether it succeeds or fails.
- Roll `2d6 + Intelligence modifier + Magic skill + Resonance study modifier`.
- The difficulty is `8 + spell level`.
- Success casts the spell normally.
- Failure expends the casting with no useful effect.
- A natural 2 also causes an album flaw, magical mishap, mutation, hostile attention, or another fitting complication.
- An unmastered track cannot be overcharged or modified by a Technique unless the GM explicitly permits it.

This rule makes familiar magic safer to improvise while keeping unfamiliar playback risky. If it produces too much spell access, remove it without changing the learning system.

## 8. Healing and niche protection

Traditionless Mages may learn spells with the Vitality Resonance, but spell-based healing should not duplicate the Partial Healer's efficiency.

Use these guardrails when adapting a healing spell:

- It consumes a normal spell casting.
- Direct restoration of hit points normally adds 1 System Strain to the recipient.
- Broad condition removal should require a higher-level spell, a long ritual, expensive components, or a serious risk.
- Restoring the dead must remain rare, costly, conditional, and adventure-defining.
- Healing Touch and other always-available Healer Arts are not ordinary album spells.

A Necromancy specialist therefore can learn healing, but the first Vitality spell will usually take longer and the resulting spell will not replace a dedicated Healer.

## Recommended first-play version

For the first several sessions, use:

1. High Mage progression without a tradition.
2. Albums as the only source of new spells.
3. Resonance modifiers applied only to learning time.
4. Mastery credits on level advancement.
5. Standard reliable casting for mastered spells.
6. No direct album casting until the group is comfortable with the basic system.

After the players understand preparation and spell slots, introduce optional unmastered playback if the group wants more volatile UVG magic.
