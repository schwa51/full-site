Choose a full class for a focused hero or choose **Adventurer** and combine two partial classes. Your class determines hit dice, attack bonus, Focus picks, and defining abilities.

## Expert

Experts are skilled problem-solvers: diplomats, healers, thieves, explorers, artisans, scholars, and other masters of a practical field.

### Masterful Expertise

Once per scene, reroll a failed non-combat skill check as an Instant action. Use the better result.

### Quick Learner

Whenever you gain a level, gain 1 extra skill point. It can be spent only on non-combat skills or raising attributes, and may be saved for later.

## Mage

Mages create carefully chosen impossibilities through spells, occult gifts, or a more physical supernatural art. Work with the GM to choose a magical tradition available in the campaign.

### Arcane Tradition

Choose one magical tradition and gain its abilities and restrictions. A Mage must obey those restrictions to use the tradition's powers.

## Psychic

Psychics are trained to channel their natural psychic potential without destroying themselves. Their powers are rare, demanding, and unlike any ordinary skill.

### Psychic training

- Choose any two psychic skills as bonus skills. You may choose the same skill twice to begin at level 1 and gain one free level-1 technique from that discipline.
- You can learn psychic disciplines and their associated techniques.
- Your maximum **Effort** is `1 + highest psychic skill + the better of Wisdom or Constitution modifier`, with a minimum of 1.

## Warrior

Warriors are exceptionally capable combatants. They have the most hit points, the strongest attack progression, and abilities that make them reliable in a lethal fight.

### Killing Blow

Whenever you deal damage with an attack, spell, or special ability, add half your character level, rounded up. This bonus also applies to Shock.

### Veteran's Luck

Once per scene as an Instant action, turn your own missed attack into a hit or turn a successful attack against you into a miss. You may use only one of those options in the scene. It cannot negate environmental damage or damage to a vehicle or mount you are riding.

## Adventurer and partial classes

An Adventurer combines any two partial classes. This creates a broader hero without the strongest defining abilities of a full Expert, Warrior, Mage, or Psychic.

### Partial Expert

Gain Quick Learner, but not Masterful Expertise.

### Partial Mage

Choose a magical tradition and gain its partial benefits and restrictions. You may choose Partial Mage twice for two different traditions; use the full Mage advancement table.

### Partial Psychic

Choose one psychic discipline and gain its skill at level 0. You may improve only that discipline and cannot learn or improve other psychic skills. Your maximum Effort uses the normal Psychic formula and is always at least 1.

### Partial Warrior

Gain the improved hit dice and attack progression shown below, but not Killing Blow or Veteran's Luck.
