# UVG + WWN Next-Session Checklists and Loadouts

Use this alongside `UVG_WWN_Player_Character_Roster.md`. The checkboxes are divided into choices players can make before the session and calculations or rulings best handled together at the table.

The recommendations below are fast-start defaults, not mandatory builds. Mage options use the campaign's traditionless **Album Mage** rules: High Mage progression underneath, UVG albums as the source of spells, and Resonance based on the spells actually mastered.

## One table-wide decision before anything else

- [ ] **Choose the attribute method for everyone.**
  - Campaign-handout method: assign `14, 12, 11, 10, 9, 7`.
  - Core WWN method apparently used on the sheets: retain the six rolled scores in order; a player who rolled may change one attribute to 14.
  - Do not let some players keep rolls while others use the array unless that is an intentional table rule.

## Dies Nox - Expert negotiator and quartermaster

### Before the next session

- [ ] Confirm the attribute method and final scores.
- [ ] Mark Origin: **Human-default**.
- [ ] Mark the citrine soul stone as a **Mystery Artifact**. Its starting function is unknown.
- [ ] Choose two Foci: one Expert/noncombat Focus and one unrestricted Focus.
  - Fast default: **Dealmaker 1** and **Diplomatic Grace 1**.
  - Dealmaker raises Trade-0 to Trade-1; Diplomatic Grace raises Convince-0 to Convince-1.
- [ ] Choose the post-background free skill.
  - Fast default: **Connect-0**.
  - Alternative: **Stab-0** if Brian wants Dies to be competent with the Gentry Wayfarer's sword.
- [ ] Confirm caravan jobs: **Negotiator** primary, **Quartermaster** backup.
- [ ] Replace "I trust Cash" with a traveler tie.
  - Ready-made option: "I trust Erik's character because she always honors a business agreement."
- [ ] Choose a personal loadout option below.

### At the beginning of the session

- [ ] GM approves the soul stone's Mystery Artifact status and confirms that it has no reliable starting power.
- [ ] Roll HP: `1d6 + Constitution modifier`, minimum 1.
- [ ] Record Expert base attack bonus: `+0`.
- [ ] Record System Strain: final Constitution score.
- [ ] Calculate Physical, Evasion, Mental, and Luck saves.
  - If the rolled array is retained: Physical 15, Evasion 14, Mental 14, Luck 15.
- [ ] Record AC, weapon hit bonuses, damage, Shock, Ready/Stowed encumbrance, cash, and languages.

### Loadout choices

**A. Road Broker - recommended**

Take the **Gentry Wayfarer** package:

- Buff coat, small shield, short sword
- Backpack, one week of rations, waterskin
- Fine clothing, writing kit and paper
- 20 silver pieces

This is the strongest match for a merchant who negotiates, keeps accounts, and needs to look prosperous. If Dies takes Connect instead of Stab, ask to swap the short sword for a crossbow and ammunition; a crossbow can be used without the normal unskilled ranged-weapon penalty.

**B. Cautious Road Agent**

Take the **Roguish Wanderer** package:

- Buff coat, small shield, short sword, five throwing blades
- Backpack, one week of rations, waterskin
- Tinder box, torches, grappling hook, and rope

Choose this if Brian expects debt collectors, back-road meetings, or discreet break-ins to matter more than fine clothes and recordkeeping. Taking Stab-0 is strongly recommended with this package.

**Useful later purchases:** lantern and oil, merchant scales or appraisal tools, a concealed reserve of cash, trade samples, and duplicate ledgers. Bulky trade goods belong on the UVG caravan sheet.

## Torron Valpin - Mage doctor and seeker of immortality

### Before the next session

- [ ] Confirm the attribute method and final scores.
- [ ] Mark Origin: **Human-default**.
- [ ] Decide whether slow aging is only story color. If it grants mechanical immunity or longevity benefits, submit it for Origin/Unique Gift review.
- [ ] Mark the Zu Complex ignition tables as a **Mystery Artifact**.
- [ ] Record the class as **Full Album Mage**; no tradition selection is required.
- [ ] Choose four level-1 starting spells and tag each with one or two Resonances.
  - Fast default: three **Necromancy** spells and one **Vitality** spell.
  - This starts Necromancy at Fluent (+1 to study related spells) and Vitality at Familiar (+0).
- [ ] Choose three of those spells to prepare and two approved general casting Techniques.
- [ ] Choose one unrestricted Focus.
  - Fast default: **Gifted Chirurgeon 1**, raising Heal-0 to Heal-1.
  - Armored alternative: **Armored Magic 1**, sacrificing the healing boost in exchange for limited armored spellcasting.
- [ ] Choose the post-background free skill.
  - Fast default: **Know-0**.
- [ ] Confirm caravan jobs: **Medic** primary, **Chronicler** backup.
- [ ] Replace "I trust no one" with a traveler tie.
  - Ready-made option: "I trust Newt Snooze because he survived my treatment and kept my secrets."
- [ ] Choose a personal loadout option below.

### At the beginning of the session

- [ ] GM approves the four starting spells, their Resonance tags, and Torron's two Techniques.
- [ ] Record Magic-0, the four mastered spells in Torron's Primer Album, three prepared spells, and one casting per day.
- [ ] Record Necromancy as Fluent if using the recommended 3/1 spell split; record the fourth spell's Resonance as Familiar.
- [ ] Roll Full Mage HP: `1d6 - 1 + Constitution modifier`, minimum 1.
- [ ] Record base attack bonus, System Strain, saves, Effort, Techniques, spell castings, and prepared spells.
  - With the rolled array: base attack +0; Physical 15, Evasion 14, Mental 15, Luck 15.
- [ ] Record AC, weapon lines, Ready/Stowed encumbrance, cash, and languages.
- [ ] Confirm that Torron is not wearing armor, bulky clothing, or holding a shield while using spells or Techniques unless he has Armored Magic.

### Loadout choices

**A. Occult Doctor-Scholar - recommended for Full Album Mage**

Take the **Mage, Healer, or Scholar** package:

- Two daggers and a staff
- Backpack; lantern, tinder box, and two flasks of oil
- Writing kit and paper
- One week of rations and a waterskin
- Healer's pouch
- 80 silver pieces

This package has every essential doctor/researcher tool and avoids armor. The staff or a dagger can remain Ready; Torron is not expected to be the party's primary combatant.

**B. Armored Field Doctor**

Take **Gentry Wayfarer** only if Torron selects Armored Magic or accepts that he must remove the buff coat and put down the shield before planned spell use. It adds a buff coat, shield, sword, fine clothing, writing supplies, and basic trail provisions.

**Useful later purchases:** additional healer's pouches, more oil, specimen jars, a shovel, a small tool, extra paper, and secure storage for the ignition tables. Medical resupply and bulky research equipment belong on the caravan sheet.

## Shared caravan loadout starter

Do this only after personal packages and caravan roles are locked.

- [ ] List all four characters and their primary/backup roles on the caravan sheet.
- [ ] Assign a Navigator or hire one.
- [ ] Enter every contributed mule, riding animal, cart, or wagon once under UVG capacity rules.
- [ ] Carry at least **8 sacks of supplies** for two weeks for four human travelers. Increase this for a longer first leg and for non-grazing animals or vehicles that consume fuel.
- [ ] Reserve one caravan gear sack for medical resupply and one for repair/animal-handling equipment if those are not abstracted into the characters' personal gear.
- [ ] Carry a minimum of one useful trade-good lot, selected after the departure point and first destination are known.
- [ ] Leave several sacks empty for discoveries, salvage, and emergency purchases.
- [ ] Decide who controls shared cash and who records debt, operating expenses, cargo value, and weekly consumption. Dies Nox is the natural default.

A practical four-person starting target is roughly **16-20 sacks of total capacity**: 8 or more supplies, several sacks of equipment and trade goods, and at least 3-5 sacks left open. The exact mix should be set after the first destination, starting season, financier, and available transport are known.

## Thirty-minute opening-session sequence

1. Resolve the attribute method for the whole group.
2. Approve Torron's and Erik's starting albums, Resonances, and casting Techniques.
3. Approve possession categories and the bacterial-colony ruling.
4. Lock Foci, free skills, and caravan roles.
5. Roll HP and finish class-specific spells, Techniques, and Effort.
6. Choose equipment packages, swaps, and Ready/Stowed items.
7. Calculate saves, AC, attacks, damage, Shock, and System Strain.
8. Replace player names or non-party answers in the trust network with character names.
9. Build the UVG caravan inventory, capacity, supplies, cash, and debt record.
