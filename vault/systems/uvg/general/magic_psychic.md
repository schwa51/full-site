## Magical Traditions

### High Mage

High Mages are scholars of the sacred physics and reality-shaping protocols left by the Long Ago. Their grimoires might be books, mnemonic tattoos, spell computers, bottled minds, or sets of ceramic calibration tablets. Whether their spells command ancient machinery, rewrite reality, or summon powers from beyond it is a question best left to philosophers and creditors.

### Elementalist

Elementalists manipulate the forces and substances from which the world is assembled: heat, wind, lightning, livingstone, water, light, and stuckforce. Some describe their work through the traditional elements, while others speak of energy gradients, material spirits, or environmental control protocols. Their magic is especially prized by travelers, engineers, farmers, and warlords.

### Necromancer

Necromancers study the separation and recombination of body, spirit, and personality: *ha*, *ka*, and *ba*. They speak with the echoes left in corpses, bind souls into vessels, awaken post-mortal servants, and repair or rearrange the machinery of death. Some are mournful physicians or ancestor-lawyers; others operate soul mills and should be shot before they explain themselves.

### Healer

Healers are biomancers who coax damaged bodies back toward a remembered ideal. Their techniques may involve symbiotic organisms, sacred surgery, medicinal radiation, diagnostic spirits, or commands whispered to the body’s microscopic maintenance systems. Their miracles are powerful, but flesh can endure only so much accelerated repair and invasive improvement.

### Vowed

The Vowed treat their own bodies as ancient artifacts awaiting restoration. Through breath, diet, meditation, pain, implanted disciplines, and impossible exercises, they rewrite reflexes, harden flesh, and awaken forgotten capacities within bone and nerve. To outsiders they may appear to be martial mystics, engineered ascetics, or dangerously self-modified athletes.

## Psychic Disciplines

### Metapsionics

Metapsions perceive and manipulate psychic power itself. They detect noöspheric activity, shield minds, suppress hostile abilities, and stabilize the talents of other psychics. The finest serve as brainguards, psychic tutors, daemon-hunters, or living circuit breakers around dangerously powerful minds.

### Precognition

Precognitives sense the branching currents of events before they settle into history. Their visions may arrive through fast-star reflections, impossible memories, prophetic migraines, or conversations with future versions of themselves. The future is never fixed, however, and every warning describes only what is most likely to happen if nobody does anything interesting.

### Telekinesis

Telekinetics grasp objects through invisible extensions of thought, manipulating weight, motion, pressure, and stuckforce. Novices perform delicate work without touching it; masters shape force into weapons, barriers, platforms, and crushing vectors. Some claim they move matter directly, while others insist they merely persuade the world that an object is already somewhere else.

### Telepathy

Telepaths touch the *ba*: the thoughts, memories, emotions, and personality patterns of living minds. They can communicate without speech, sense intentions, uncover secrets, and eventually alter perception or memory. Even where psychics are tolerated, telepaths inspire fear, elaborate etiquette, and a thriving market in mirrored helmets and fraudulent mind wards.
