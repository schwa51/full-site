## Armor

### Armor traits

- **Cool:** No heat penalty. After a cold night without a blanket or sheltered camp, make a Physical save; on a failure, recover no System Strain from that night's rest.
- **Cumbersome:** Apply the armor's Enc as a penalty to physical Sneak and Exert checks.
- **Environmental:** Gain +2 on saves and skill checks against environmental heat, cold, acid, toxic air, contaminated water, and similar exposure. A sealed suit also blocks mundane inhaled hazards while its air supply lasts.
- **Hot:** After a fight or other strenuous scene in hot weather, the wearer must rest ten minutes and drink water. If they cannot, they gain 1 System Strain. If already at maximum Strain, they instead take -1 on physical checks and saves until they rest and drink.
- **Intravenous:** Once per round, a Readied potion, drug, or elixir can be injected as an On Turn action rather than used as a Main Action.
- **Powered #**: The suit begins with the listed charges and expends 1 after every combat or scene of major exertion. As an On Turn action before rolling, the wearer may spend an additional charge for +2 on one attack roll, Physical or Evasion save, or opposed combat skill check that round. At 0 charges, AC remains but other powered traits shut down. A recharge cradle or spare source costs €500, is Enc 5, and restores all charges overnight.
- **Adaptive:** After seven days in one broad environment, the suit gains Environmental against that environment. It loses the prior adaptation. It must be fed one day's living ration each week or this benefit shuts down.

## Weapons

Melee attacks use Stab unless an entry says Punch. Ranged attacks use Shoot. A thrown weapon may use Stab or Shoot.

### Weapon traits

WWN traits retain their normal rules: **2H** (two-handed), **AP** (ignore nonmagical armor and shields for the hit roll), **L** (long/reach), **LL** (less lethal), **PM** (precisely murderous), **R** (Move Action to reload, or On Turn with Shoot-1), **S** (subtle), **SR** (Main Action to reload), and **T** (throwable).

The following traits are added for UVG weapons:

- **Blinding:** If any damage die rolls its maximum, the target is blinded for one round: it cannot make ranged attacks and rolls melee attacks twice, taking the worse result. On a natural 20 that also triggers Blinding, the target must make a Physical save or the blindness is lasting until repaired or magically cured.
- **Burst:** As a Main Action, empty the weapon at a point within short range. Every target in a 10-foot radius makes an Evasion save, taking normal weapon damage on a failure or half on a success. A target in hard cover takes no damage on a success. The weapon becomes depleted.
- **D# (Depletion):** After an attack roll showing the listed natural number or lower, the weapon is empty or overheated after resolving the shot. Reloading or cooling it takes a Main Action; Deadeye-2 can do so as an On Turn action. A standard ammunition pack is Enc 1, costs one-tenth the weapon's price, and provides three full reloads.
- **Frag:** A creature killed by the attack explodes, dealing 1d6 damage to everything within 10 feet; an Evasion save halves this collateral damage.
- **Intravenous:** One dose of a liquid toxin, drug, holy water, or potion can be loaded. A hit delivers it in addition to weapon damage, allowing any normal save.
- **Mounted:** One-handed while mounted and two-handed on foot. A mounted charge that hits deals double damage unless the entry says otherwise.
- **Stun 20:** On a natural 20, the target makes a Physical save. On a failure, it loses its next Main Action.
- **V (Versatile):** One-handed, or two-handed for the second listed damage and Shock values.
- **Exploding (`*`):** When a starred damage die rolls its maximum, roll another die of the same type and add it. Further maximum results continue exploding.
- **Rare:** Availability is controlled by setting and faction access, not merely price.

### Bows and gun-wands

Ranged weapons do no Shock. Firearm, beam, and gun-wand ammunition follows the D# ammunition-pack rule; arrows and quarrels use normal WWN ammunition bundles.
